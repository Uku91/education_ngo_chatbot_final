# HOPEEDUCATION FOUNDATION - KNOWLEDGE BASE
# Location: Abuja, Nigeria

== ABOUT US ==
HopeEducation Foundation is a non-profit NGO founded in 2018 in Abuja, FCT.
Mission: To ensure every child in underserved communities in Abuja has access to quality basic education.
Vision: An Abuja where no child is out of school due to poverty.
Registration: CAC/IT/No 123456
Office: Suite 12, 2nd Floor, Emeka Anyaoku Street, Area 10, Garki, Abuja.
Hours: Monday-Friday, 9am - 4pm. Closed weekends & public holidays.
Contact: +234 803 123 4567, info@hopeducation.ng, www.hopeeducation.ng

== OUR PROGRAMS ==
1. BACK-TO-SCHOOL SCHOLARSHIP:
   - For children ages 6-15 in public primary schools in Bwari, Kuje, Kwali Area Councils.
   - Covers: school fees, uniforms, books, sandals, bag.
   - Eligibility: Family income < N50,000/month, child out of school or at risk of dropping out.
   - Application period: June 1 - August 31 every year. Apply via website or office.

2. GIRLS-LEARN INITIATIVE:
   - Supports 100 girls yearly in JSS1-JSS3 with mentorship, sanitary pads, after-school tutoring in Maths & English.
   - Centers: Garki, Lugbe, Kubwa.

3. COMMUNITY LEARNING HUBS:
   - Free after-school lessons Mon-Thu 3:30pm-5:30pm for Primary 4 to JSS3.
   - Subjects: English, Mathematics, Basic Science.
   - Locations: Lugbe IDP camp, Mpape, Jikwoyi.

4. TEACHER TRAINING:
   - Quarterly training for low-cost private school teachers.

== HOW TO GET HELP (FOR PARENTS/GUARDIANS) ==
If your child needs support:
1. Visit our office or call +234 803 123 4567
2. Bring: Child's birth certificate, 1 passport photo, proof of residence (NEPA bill), last school result if any.
3. Fill the Beneficiary Form.
4. We will verify within 2 weeks.
We DO NOT give cash. We pay directly to school or provide materials.

== HOW TO VOLUNTEER ==
We need: Tutors (Maths/English), Mentors for girls, Fundraisers, Content creators.
Requirements: 18 years+, commit at least 4 hours/week for 3 months.
How to apply: Fill volunteer form on website: www.hopeeducation.ng/volunteer OR email volunteer@hopeeducation.ng with CV and why you want to volunteer.
We provide certificate after 3 months.

== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ==
Q: Is HopeLearn free?
A: Yes, all our programs for children are 100% free for beneficiaries.

Q: Do you cover secondary school or university?
A: Currently we only cover Primary and Junior Secondary (JSS). Senior Secondary only for our Girls-Education graduates.

Q: Are you in Lagos or other states?
A: No, we currently operate only in FCT Abuja - Bwari, Kuje, Kwali, AMAC.

Q: Can I sponsor a specific child?
A: Yes, through our Child Sponsor Program - N120,000 per year covers one child fully. You get quarterly reports and letters. Email sponsor@hopeducation.ng

Q: Do you do school outreach?
A: Yes, schools can invite us for career talks or girls sensitization. Request 2 weeks ahead via info@hopeeducation.ng

Q: I donated but didn't get receipt?
A: Please forward proof of payment to finance@hopelearn.ng and WhatsApp +234 803 123 4567

== SAFEGUARDING POLICY ==
We have zero tolerance for abuse. All staff and volunteers are screened. If you have a concern about a child, email safe@hopeducation.ng - confidential.
